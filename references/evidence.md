# Evidence fields

- `reports`: recent completed daily digests and their selected materials.
- `videos`: up to 12 visible, non-duplicate, completed analyses matched to the question.
- `evaluation`: evidence for information delivery, visual harmony, and audiovisual coherence.
- `highlights`: notable time-coded moments confirmed by native video analysis.
- `developer` and `publisher`: show only when confirmed; empty means unknown and must be omitted.
- `url`: original platform page to cite in the reply.

The library excludes vertical, character/hero, interview, behind-the-scenes, ordinary short-cut, and pure-gameplay materials according to the Radar’s current curation rules.
